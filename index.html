FIELD GUIDE

How to Play

1 Play poker. Deal damage.

Pick up to 5 cards and play a poker hand. Your Damage × Mult becomes the hit. Drain the boss's HP before you run out of hands.

2 Build the deck.

Each card's rank adds Damage. Gems add Damage or Mult. Buy gem caches at the shop to permanently tip more cards.

3 Collect accessories.

Accessories are this world's jokers. They trigger every hand. Five slots, dozens of synergies, one very angry moon.

4 Read the boss.

Every boss warps the rules: dead suits, disabled accessories, smaller hands. Build around the next fight, not the last one.
